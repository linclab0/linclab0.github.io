Language, Interaction, & Cognition Lab

Department of Cognitive & Information Sciences

How do we decode another person’s thoughts and intentions from a sequence of sounds (speech) or shapes (letters, signs)? And how do we choose what to say next to transfer our thoughts to another person’s mind? The LInC Lab is a group of cognitive scientists tackling these questions using behavioral, neuroscientific, and computational tools.

To get more information about ongoing projects, please visit the research page or check individual lab members’ pages for a full list of their publications. If you would like to participate in the lab’s research or are interested in joining the lab, please get in touch.
